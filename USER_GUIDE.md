## 1. Overview

Valoxi is a cloud-based platform built for acquisition professionals — whether you are completing your first deal or managing a portfolio of live processes simultaneously. It brings together every stage of the M&A lifecycle in one place:

- **Deal sourcing and pipeline management** — track targets from first contact through to completion using a visual Kanban board.
- **AI-powered document screening** — upload financial documents and let Valoxi extract, classify, and analyse the data automatically.
- **Expert Analysis** — receive a structured, multi-dimensional assessment of each target, covering financials, operations, risk, sector dynamics, valuation, and more.
- **Deal Modeller** — build and version your deal structure, including enterprise value, funding mix, and deferred payment terms.
- **Value Creation** — model post-acquisition growth, run scenario analysis, and assess projected returns (IRR and cash-on-cash multiple).
- **Seller Portal** — send a secure link to the target company so they can upload documents and sign an NDA directly, without requiring a Valoxi account.

Valoxi is designed for a professional audience and requires no technical knowledge to use.

## 2. Getting Started

### Signing In

Navigate to your Valoxi URL and sign in with your email and password. If you have forgotten your password, use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page to receive a reset email.

If your organisation uses a magic link login, check your inbox for the sign-in email and click the link to access the platform instantly.

### Onboarding

New users are guided through a short onboarding flow the first time they sign in. This sets up your company profile and confirms the key settings needed to personalise your workspace.

### Navigation

The left-hand sidebar gives you access to every major section of the platform:

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | High-level overview of your activity and key metrics |
| Pipeline | Visual Kanban board of all your active deals |
| Target Companies | Full list of all targets you are tracking |
| Portal Management | Manage Seller Portal links and track document submissions |
| Contacts | Directory of individuals linked to your deals |
| Team | Invite and manage team members |
| Notifications | Activity alerts and reminders |
| Billing | Manage your subscription and payment details |
| Account | Update your profile and company settings |

## 3. Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen. It gives you an instant snapshot of where your deal activity stands.

### Key Metrics

At the top of the page, four summary cards show:

- **Target Companies** — the number of active targets currently in your pipeline.
- **Total Deals** — the total number of companies you have ever tracked, including archived ones.
- **Offers Sent** — the number of targets that have reached the Offer Stage or beyond.
- **Total Pipeline Value** — the combined estimated deal value of all active targets.

### Dashboard Widgets

Beneath the metrics, several widgets surface the information most relevant to your day-to-day work:

- **Pipeline Overview** — a visual breakdown of how many deals sit in each stage of your pipeline.
- **Hot Deals** — targets flagged as high priority that need your attention soon.
- **Upcoming Reminders** — tasks and deadlines you have set against specific targets.
- **Stage Alerts** — deals that have been sitting in the same stage for an unusually long time, prompting you to take action.
- **Recent Activity / Team Activity** — a running log of updates across your workspace, including actions taken by team members.

### Quick Actions

Buttons on the Dashboard let you jump straight to adding a new target company, viewing the full pipeline, or managing your team without navigating the sidebar.

## 4. Pipeline

The Pipeline page gives you a Kanban-style view of your entire deal flow. Each column represents a stage in the acquisition process, and each card represents a target company.

### Deal Stages

The default pipeline stages move a deal from initial identification through to completion:

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| New Target | Newly identified company you are considering |
| Initial Contact | You have made first contact with the owner or their representative |
| Documents Requested | You have asked the target to share financial and operational documents |
| Under Review | You are actively reviewing the documents and business |
| Offer Stage | You are preparing or have submitted an offer |
| Heads of Terms | You are negotiating and agreeing the headline deal terms |
| Due Diligence | Formal legal and financial due diligence is underway |
| Legal & Accounting | Legal and accounting workstreams are active |
| Completion | The deal is completing or has completed |
| Not Qualified | The target did not meet your criteria and has been closed out |

### Moving Deals Through the Pipeline

Drag a deal card from one column to another to update its stage. The platform records the transition and can trigger automated actions — for example, when you create a Seller Portal link for a target, the deal may automatically advance to the Documents Requested stage.

### Adding a New Target from the Pipeline

Use the **Add Company** button at the top of the Pipeline page to create a new target directly. You will be prompted to enter the company name and any initial details before the card appears in the pipeline.

## 5. Target Companies

The Target Companies page lists every company you are tracking. Click on any company name to open its full profile, which is organised into tabs.

### 5.1 Adding a Target

Click **Add Company** (available from the Pipeline, Dashboard, or Target Companies list) and fill in the company's basic details: name, sector, approximate revenue, and any notes. Once created, the target appears in your pipeline and you can begin building the deal file.

### 5.2 Uploading Documents

The **Documents & DD** section within a target profile is where you upload the financial and operational documents that power Valoxi's analysis.

Supported document types include:

- Profit & Loss statement
- Balance Sheet
- Management accounts
- Asset register
- Sales ledger
- And other standard due diligence materials

**How to upload:** Navigate to the target company, go to the Documents & DD tab, and drag and drop files or click to browse. Valoxi accepts common file formats including PDF, Excel, and Word documents.

**What happens next — the AI screening workflow:**

1. **Classification** — Valoxi automatically identifies the type of each document (e.g. "Profit & Loss Statement") using AI.
2. **Extraction** — Structured financial data is extracted from the document and saved.
3. **Normalisation** — Revenue, cost, and balance sheet figures are standardised so they can feed directly into the Deal Modeller.
4. **Phase 1 Screening** — Once all required document types have been uploaded and processed, Valoxi automatically triggers the first stage of Expert Analysis:
   - A **Data Quality** check validates whether the extracted data is complete and reliable, assigning a quality score.
   - A **Screening** assessment produces an initial viability verdict: **Strong Proceed**, **Proceed**, or **No Go**.

You do not need to trigger these steps manually — they run automatically as soon as the required documents are in place.

### 5.3 Expert Analysis

The **Expert Analysis** tab provides a structured, AI-generated assessment of the target company across multiple dimensions. This is available on the Professional plan and above.

Analysis is organised in layers, each building on the outputs of the previous:

| Layer | Analysis |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Data Quality, Initial Screening |
| Layer 1 | Financial, Operational, Risk |
| Layer 2 | Quality of Earnings, Sector |
| Layer 3 | Valuation, Deal Structure |
| Layer 4 | Synthesis (overall recommendation) |

Each dimension produces a written assessment with supporting data points, ratings, and flags. The **Explain** button next to any finding lets you ask the AI a follow-up question to get deeper context on a specific point — useful when you want to probe a risk or understand an assumption.

The **Synthesis** output at the end brings all dimensions together into a final deal verdict and overall recommendation.

### 5.4 Deal Modeller

The **Deal Modeller** tab is where you structure the financial terms of the deal. It is available in a simplified form on the Core plan, and in its full version on Professional and above.

Key sections include:

- **Enterprise Value** — set your target enterprise value and adjust the assumptions that drive it (e.g. EBITDA multiple, revenue multiple).
- **Funding Structure** — define how the deal will be funded, including cash at completion, bank debt, deferred payments, and earn-outs.
- **Offer Structuring** — model the cash consideration payable to the seller at different funding scenarios.
- **P&L Summary** — see the projected impact of the deal terms on the combined business's profitability in the years following completion.
- **Viability** — an assessment of whether the business can service its post-acquisition debt obligations, including key coverage ratios.

On the Professional plan, you can save multiple versions of your deal model (up to 5 versions, with 90 days of history) so you can compare different scenarios side by side and track how your thinking has evolved. The Expert plan increases this to 20 versions with 180 days of history.

### 5.5 Value Creation

The **Value Creation** tab focuses on what the business could be worth after your ownership, rather than at the point of acquisition. This tab is available on the Expert plan.

Key features include:

- **Growth Modelling** — set assumptions for revenue growth, margin improvement, and cost synergies across a 5-year projection period.
- **Scenario Analysis** — test how sensitive your projected returns are to changes in key inputs (e.g. if revenue growth is 2% lower than expected, how does that affect exit value?). Results are displayed as a heat map so you can see the range of outcomes at a glance.
- **Exit Scenario** — model the likely exit value of the business at the end of your hold period, based on prevailing market multiples.
- **Returns** — view projected IRR (Internal Rate of Return) and MoM (Money-on-Money multiple, also referred to as MOIC) across different scenarios.
- **Waterfall** — a visual breakdown of how exit proceeds would be distributed, reflecting the priority order of different financing instruments.

## 6. Seller Portal

The Seller Portal is a secure, standalone web page that you send to a contact at the target company. It allows them to upload documents and (if required) sign an NDA — without needing to create a Valoxi account. Everything they submit feeds directly into the target company profile in your workspace.

### 6.1 Setting Up a Portal Link

1. Go to **Portal Management** in the left-hand sidebar.
2. Click **Create Portal Link** and select the target company and the primary contact you want to send it to.
3. Optionally add a personalised message that the contact will see when they open the portal.
4. Choose whether to require the contact to sign a **Non-Disclosure Agreement** before they can upload any documents.
5. Click **Generate Link**. Valoxi sends an email to the contact with their secure, unique access link.

Each link is time-limited and tied to a specific contact, so you maintain full control over who can access the portal and for how long.

### 6.2 NDA Signing

If you enabled NDA signing when creating the portal link, the contact must complete this step before they can upload any documents.

The process from the contact's perspective:

1. They open their portal link and are presented with the NDA document.
2. They must scroll through the full document before the acceptance option becomes available — this ensures they have had the opportunity to read the terms.
3. They check the agreement box and type their full name as their electronic signature.
4. The system records their name, the timestamp, and their IP address.
5. A signed PDF copy is generated automatically and made available to download immediately. A copy is also stored securely in the platform.

Once the NDA is signed, the contact proceeds to the document upload section.

### 6.3 Document Submission (Seller View)

Inside the portal, the contact sees a structured checklist of the documents you are requesting. Each category (e.g. "Profit & Loss", "Balance Sheet", "Asset Register") has a clearly labelled upload area.

The contact uploads files by dragging them into the relevant category or clicking to browse their computer. A progress bar at the top of the page shows how many of the required document types have been successfully submitted and processed, giving the seller a clear sense of completion.

### 6.4 Monitoring Progress

Once a contact has been given portal access, you can track their progress from inside the target company profile and from the Portal Management page. You will see which document types have been submitted, which are still outstanding, and whether the AI has successfully processed each file.

**Automated chase-ups:** You can configure the portal to send automatic reminder emails to the contact if they have not completed their submissions within a set number of days. The system sends a gentle reminder first, escalating to a more urgent follow-up if documents remain outstanding after the second deadline.

## 7. Portal Management

The **Portal Management** page gives you a central view of all the Seller Portal links you have created across all your target companies.

From this page you can:

- See the total number of portal links you have active.
- Track how many contacts have submitted documents.
- Review the status of each individual portal link (active, expired, or completed).
- Create new portal links directly from the page.
- Delete or deactivate links that are no longer needed.

This page is especially useful when you have multiple live deal processes running simultaneously and need a consolidated view of where each target is in the document collection process.

## 8. Contacts and Team

### Contacts

The **Contacts** page is a directory of all the individuals linked to your deals — founders, business owners, advisers, and any other contacts you have added. You can associate contacts with specific target companies and record their role in the process.

### Team

The **Team** page allows company admins to invite colleagues and manage their access to the workspace. To invite a new team member:

1. Go to **Team** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Invite Member** and enter their email address.
3. They will receive an invitation email with a link to set up their account.

Team members can collaborate on target company profiles, view the pipeline, and contribute to deal analysis. The number of seats available depends on your subscription plan.

---

## 9. Account Settings

The **Account** page lets you manage your personal profile and (for admins) your organisation's settings.

### Profile

Update your first name, last name, and job title from the Profile section.

To change your password, use the **Security** section on the same page. If your organisation uses Clerk for authentication, you can also manage your active sessions and connected accounts from here.

### Company Settings (Admins only)

Company administrators can update:

- **Company Name**
- **Industry** — select from a predefined list of sectors.
- **Company Size**
- **Country of Operation** — UK or US (this affects certain financial formatting and regulatory references throughout the platform).
- **Business contact details** — email, phone, and address.

Changes to company settings are applied across the entire workspace for all team members.

## 10. Billing and Subscription Plans

The **Billing** page is where you manage your Valoxi subscription, view your invoices, and update your payment method.

### Subscription Tiers

Valoxi offers three plans, each designed for a different level of deal activity:

#### Core — from $87/month

Best for solo buyers managing their first acquisition.

- Up to 5 active target companies
- 1 GB document storage
- Pipeline Kanban board
- AI document extraction and classification
- Simplified Deal Modeller (single version, no history)
- AI-generated offer letters
- Seller Portal (document collection and NDA)
- Email support and guided deal checklist

#### Professional — from $167/month

Best for active acquirers with multiple live deal processes.

Includes everything in Core, plus:

- Up to 15 active target companies
- 10 GB document storage
- Full Deal Modeller with up to 5 saved versions (90-day history)
- Expert Deep-Dive Analysis across all 10 analytical dimensions
- DD Data Room with review workflows
- Onboarding call and priority feature access

#### Expert — from $397/month

Best for M&A professionals and investment firms running high-volume or complex transactions.

Includes everything in Professional, plus:

- Up to 50 active target companies
- 25 GB document storage
- Deal Modeller with up to 20 saved versions (180-day history)
- Value Creation Modeller with sensitivity heat maps
- Heads of Terms builder
- White-label Seller Portal (your branding, not Valoxi's)
- API access for custom integrations
- Priority support

Annual billing is available at a 20% discount across all plans.

### Add-ons

If you need more than your plan includes, you can purchase additional capacity:

- **Extra Seats** — add more team members beyond the plan default.
- **Extra Company Slots** — add more active target slots if you exceed your plan's limit.

### Managing Your Subscription

From the Billing page, you can:

- View your current plan and renewal date.
- Upgrade or downgrade your plan.
- Add or remove seats and company slots.
- Update your payment method.
- Download past invoices.

Clicking **Manage Subscription** opens the Stripe customer portal, where all payment-related actions are handled securely.

### Promotional Benefits

If you joined Valoxi during a promotional period (such as an Early Bird offer or community partnership), any active benefits and their expiry dates are displayed at the top of the Billing page.
