# Deal Sheet Data Extraction Requirements

## 1. FROM STATUTORY ACCOUNTS (Historic - 3-5 years)

### Profit & Loss Statement
- **Revenues** (turnover)
- **Cost of Sales**
- **Administrative Expenses** (total figure)
- **Depreciation & Amortisation** (usually in notes)
- **Other Operating Income**
- **Interest Paid/Received**
- **Profit Before Tax**
- **Corporation Tax Charge**
- **Net Profit**

### Balance Sheet
**Fixed Assets:**
- Intangible Assets (goodwill, brand, IP)
- Tangible Fixed Assets - broken down by:
  - Freehold Property
  - Leasehold Property
  - Plant & Machinery
  - Fixtures & Fittings
  - Motor Vehicles
  - Computer Equipment

**Current Assets:**
- Stock/Inventory
- Trade Debtors (Receivables)
- Prepayments
- Amounts Owed by Directors
- Other Debtors
- Cash at Bank and in Hand

**Current Liabilities:**
- Trade Creditors (Payables)
- Taxation and Social Security (PAYE, NI, VAT)
- Other Taxation (Corporation Tax)
- Bank Loans & Overdrafts (current portion)
- Accruals
- Amounts Owed to Directors
- Other Creditors

**Long-term Liabilities:**
- Bank Loans (non-current)
- Deferred Taxation
- Other Long-term Debt

**Equity:**
- Share Capital
- Retained Earnings/Profit & Loss Reserves

### Notes to Accounts
- Detailed depreciation policy and rates
- Details of director remuneration
- Details of exceptional/non-recurring items
- Details of borrowings (lender, rate, term)

---

## 2. FROM CURRENT MANAGEMENT ACCOUNTS (Most Recent Period)

### Profit & Loss (YTD or Latest Month/Quarter)
- Revenues
- Cost of Sales
- Gross Profit
- Administrative Expenses
- Depreciation & Amortisation
- Other Operating Income
- Interest Paid/Received
- Profit Before Tax
- Tax Charge
- Net Profit

### Balance Sheet (As at latest date)
**All the same line items as statutory accounts:**
- All Fixed Assets categories
- All Current Assets
- All Current Liabilities  
- All Long-term Liabilities
- Equity position

---

## 3. FROM CURRENT PROFIT & LOSS STATEMENT (Detailed/Management Version)

### Revenue Detail
- Total Revenue/Turnover
- Revenue by product/service line (if available)
- Revenue by customer segment (if available)

### Cost of Sales Breakdown
- Direct Materials
- Direct Labour
- Subcontractor Costs
- Other Direct Costs

### Overheads/Administrative Expenses Detail
- Staff Costs (wages, salaries, NI, pension)
- Rent & Rates
- Utilities
- Insurance
- Professional Fees
- Marketing & Advertising
- IT Costs
- Vehicle Costs
- Travel & Subsistence
- Office Costs
- Repairs & Maintenance
- Bank Charges
- Depreciation
- Amortisation
- Other Overheads

### Other Items
- Other Operating Income
- Interest Income
- Interest Expense/Bank Charges

---

## 4. FROM CURRENT BALANCE SHEET (Detailed Version)

**Fixed Assets with Cost & Depreciation:**
- Gross Book Value (original cost)
- Accumulated Depreciation
- Net Book Value
- For each asset category

**Current Assets Detail:**
- Stock/Inventory valuation
- Trade Debtors total
- Prepayments detail
- Director Loan Accounts (debit balances)
- VAT Debtor
- Other Debtors
- Cash - broken down by account if multiple

**Current Liabilities Detail:**
- Trade Creditors total
- PAYE/NI Liability
- VAT Liability
- Corporation Tax Due
- Director Loan Accounts (credit balances)
- Accruals (broken down if detailed)
- Bank Overdraft/Short-term Loans
- Current Portion of Long-term Debt

**Long-term Liabilities Detail:**
- Each loan facility separately:
  - Outstanding balance
  - Lender name
  - Interest rate
  - Repayment terms
  - Security held
- Deferred Tax balance

---

## 5. FROM STAFF MATRIX/PAYROLL REPORT

### For Each Employee/Director:
- Name/Position
- Gross Salary/Wage
- Employer NI Contribution
- Employer Pension Contribution
- Total Employment Cost
- Role (Director/Owner vs Employee)

### Summary Data:
- Total Staff Costs
- Number of Employees
- Director/Owner total remuneration
- Split between:
  - Owner/Working Directors
  - Non-working Directors
  - Employees

---

## 6. FROM SALES LEDGER (Aged Debtors Report)

### Aged Receivables Analysis:
- Total Trade Debtors
- Aged breakdown:
  - Current (0-30 days)
  - 31-60 days
  - 61-90 days
  - 90+ days
- Bad debt provision/doubtful debts
- Largest debtor balances (top 10)

### Debtor Days Calculation Data:
- Total receivables balance
- Recent revenue figure (for days calculation)

---

## 7. FROM PURCHASE LEDGER (Aged Creditors Report)

### Aged Payables Analysis:
- Total Trade Creditors
- Aged breakdown:
  - Current (0-30 days)
  - 31-60 days
  - 61-90 days
  - 90+ days
- Largest creditor balances (top 10)

### Creditor Days Calculation Data:
- Total payables balance
- Recent cost of sales/purchases figure

---

## 8. FROM FIXED ASSET REGISTER

### For Each Asset:
- Asset Description
- Asset Category (Property/Plant/Vehicle/IT etc)
- Date of Purchase
- Original Cost
- Accumulated Depreciation
- Net Book Value
- Depreciation Rate/Method
- Location
- Condition

### Refinanceable Assets Specifically:
- **Property:**
  - Freehold/Leasehold status
  - Address
  - Market valuation (if recent)
  - Original purchase price
  - Net book value
  
- **Plant & Machinery:**
  - Equipment description
  - Age
  - Condition
  - Original cost
  - Current NBV
  - Market/resale value estimate
  
- **Motor Vehicles:**
  - Make/Model
  - Registration
  - Purchase date/price
  - Current NBV
  - Estimated market value

### Summary Data:
- Total Fixed Assets by Category
- Total Accumulated Depreciation
- Net Book Value
- Assets suitable for refinancing
- Assets fully depreciated but still in use

---

## 9. FROM STOCK/INVENTORY RECORDS

### Stock Valuation:
- Total Stock Value
- Stock by Category:
  - Raw Materials
  - Work in Progress
  - Finished Goods
- Stock Movement (if available):
  - Opening Stock
  - Purchases
  - Closing Stock

### Inventory Days Data:
- Average stock holding value
- Cost of sales (for calculation)

---

## 10. FROM LOAN/DEBT DOCUMENTATION

### For Each Facility:
- Lender Name
- Loan Type (Term Loan/Asset Finance/Property Mortgage/Overdraft)
- Original Amount Advanced
- Current Outstanding Balance
- Interest Rate (%)
- Term (total months/years)
- Start Date
- Maturity Date
- Monthly/Annual Repayment Amount
- Security/Collateral
- Personal Guarantees
- Covenants
- Arrangement/Setup Fees Paid
- Early Repayment Terms/Penalties

---

## KEY DATES REQUIRED

- Latest Statutory Accounts Date
- Latest Management Accounts Date
- Current Balance Sheet Date
- Aged Debtors/Creditors Report Date
- Fixed Asset Register Date
- Stock Valuation Date

---

## NOTES

**This list contains ONLY raw data that can be directly extracted from documents.**

**Excluded (as these are calculations):**
- Gross Profit (calculated from Revenue - Cost of Sales)
- EBITDA (calculated)
- Net Current Assets (calculated)
- Working Capital (calculated)
- Debt ratios (calculated)
- Margin percentages (calculated)
- Growth rates (calculated)
- Normalised/Adjusted EBITDA (calculated)
- Valuation multiples (judgement/market based)
- Enterprise value (calculated)
- LTV ratios (calculated)
- Payment cover (calculated)
