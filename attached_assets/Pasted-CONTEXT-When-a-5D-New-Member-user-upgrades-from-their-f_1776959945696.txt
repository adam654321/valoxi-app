CONTEXT
When a 5D New Member user upgrades from their free Core period via POST /api/v2/billing/checkout, Stripe currently receives BOTH (a) the intended customer balance credit of unused_months × $73.95, and (b) an unintended trial_period_days equal to the remaining free-period days. This stacks the benefits: trial covers the first ~5 months, then credit covers another ~2.5 months. Designed behaviour is credit-only — user is charged immediately at Pro × 0.85, credit depletes prorata against invoices until exhausted. Root cause: the trial-extension block at the end of /checkout guards on !existingSub.promoCodeId, but 5D free-month state lives on users.assignedPromoCampaignId, not on subscriptions.promoCodeId. The guard passes and trial extension fires on top of the credit block. Evidence: invoice OIKFFCPJ-0001 dated 23 Apr 2026 for user_company_id 74 shows "Trial period for Professional, Apr 23 to Sep 5, 2026" at $0 alongside a $369.75 customer balance credit.

GOAL
Add a single predicate to the trial-extension if-condition inside POST /checkout so users with active free-month state skip trial_period_days. One file, one logical change. Do not touch the credit block, the /upgrade handler, or any webhook handler.
