[DESIGN] When the buyer clicks "Progress to Analysis" or "Re-run Analysis"
on the Documents page AND there is an active+incomplete chase-up schedule
for this targetCompanyId AND completedCount < 10, an AlertDialog appears
with:
- The existing incomplete-docs warning text (already present on Progress
  to Analysis path)
- A new section: a short heading and a radio group with two options
- "Continue chasing" pre-selected, "Stop chases now" as the alternative
- Confirm action: if "Stop chases now" selected, call manual-stop endpoint
  first, then fire analysis trigger. If "Continue chasing", just fire
  analysis trigger.
- Cancel: dismiss without firing anything.

For Re-run, this is a new AlertDialog wrap (currently the Re-run button
has no dialog). Same content and conditions.

If completedCount === 10 OR no active schedule, no dialog appears.
Existing behaviour preserved.

"Active schedule" means is_active=true AND is_completed=false for this
targetCompanyId.
