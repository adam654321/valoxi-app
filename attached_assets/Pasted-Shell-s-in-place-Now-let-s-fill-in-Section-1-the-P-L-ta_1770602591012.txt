Visual treatment:

The EBITDA row should be visually emphasised — bold text, slightly different background (e.g. bg-primary/5)
Margin % rows should be grey italic, slightly smaller — they're reference info, not primary data
The separator before Directors' Remuneration marks the shift from P&L to supplementary info
Negative values should be shown in parentheses: (£45,000) rather than -£45,000 — this is accounting convention
Columns for years included in the averaging period should have a subtle highlight (e.g. very light blue background) to show the user which years are feeding into the EBITDA calculation
