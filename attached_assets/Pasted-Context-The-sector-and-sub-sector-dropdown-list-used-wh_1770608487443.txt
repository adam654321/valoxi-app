Context
The sector and sub-sector dropdown list used when creating target companies is too limited for UK SME M&A deal flow. It has 15 generic sectors with exactly 5 sub-sectors each. Many common UK SME acquisition targets (care homes, IT managed services, trade businesses, cleaning companies, motor trade, etc.) don't fit neatly into the current list. The sector choice feeds directly into the Expert Analysis engine (sector expert prompt, valuation expert prompt) so accuracy here affects the quality of the entire analysis.
