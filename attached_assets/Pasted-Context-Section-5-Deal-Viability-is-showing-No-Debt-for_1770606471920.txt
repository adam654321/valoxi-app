Context
Section 5 (Deal Viability) is showing "No Debt" for the Payment Cover Ratio even when funding lines with debt terms are active. This is a bug — the active funding lines' annual repayments should be feeding into the payment cover calculation. The DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) also needs to be prominently displayed — this is the metric lenders look at first when assessing whether a deal is fundable.

Enhancement: DSCR Display
Lenders think in terms of DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio). It's the same calculation as payment cover ratio (Adjusted EBITDA / Total Annual Debt Service) but needs to be presented with lending context. Update the viability section to include:

Rename "Payment Cover Ratio" to "Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)" — this is the industry-standard term. You can show "Also known as Payment Cover Ratio" as a subtitle if you want, but DSCR should be the primary label.
Show the DSCR formula explicitly:

   DSCR = Adjusted EBITDA (£X) ÷ Annual Debt Service (£X) = X.Xx
This helps the user (and their lender) see exactly what's going into the ratio.

Show the annual debt service breakdown:

Lender context notes — Below the DSCR display, add practical guidance:

"Most lenders require a minimum DSCR of 1.25x-1.50x for senior debt"
"A DSCR above 2.0x provides comfortable headroom for covenant compliance"
"DSCR below 1.0x means the business cannot service its debt from operating cash flow"

These should be subtle info notes, not prominent warnings — the gauge bar already communicates the status visually.
