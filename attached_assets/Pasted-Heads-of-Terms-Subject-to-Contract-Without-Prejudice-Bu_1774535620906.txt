1. The Proposed Acquisition
The Buyer proposes to acquire 100% of the share capital of Macinnes Tooling ("the Company") on the terms set out below.

2. Price
The total consideration for the Shares shall be £890,421 ("the Price"), subject to adjustment as described below.

Enterprise Value (Adjusted EBITDA × 2.2 multiple): £725,171
Plus Excess Cash: £384,000
Less Working Capital Adjustment: (£218,750)
Total Offer Price: £890,421
3. Payment Structure
Payment due to the seller at closing: £620,696

Deferred consideration: £269,725

Payable at: £5,619 per month over 48 months

4. Key Terms
Exclusivity Period	120 days from the date of these Heads of Terms
Non-Compete Restriction	3 years post-completion
Non-Solicitation Restriction	3 years post-completion
Warranty Claim Period	18 months post-completion
Minimum Warranty Claim	£8,000
Target Completion	12 weeks from signing
5. Conditions
This acquisition is conditional upon:

Satisfactory completion of due diligence by the Buyer
Agreement and execution of a Share Purchase Agreement
No material adverse change in the business prior to completion
Receipt of all necessary regulatory approvals and third-party consents
6. Due Diligence
The Buyer shall be entitled to conduct full financial, legal, commercial, and operational due diligence on the Company during the Exclusivity Period. The Seller agrees to provide reasonable access to all requested information and documentation.

7. Exclusivity
The Seller agrees that for a period of 120 days from the date of these Heads of Terms, they shall not directly or indirectly solicit, encourage, or negotiate with any third party regarding the sale of the Company or any material part of its business or assets.

8. Timetable
The parties shall use reasonable endeavours to complete the transaction within 12 weeks from the date of these Heads of Terms, subject to satisfactory completion of due diligence and agreement of the Share Purchase Agreement.

9. Costs
Each party shall bear their own costs in relation to the negotiation and completion of the proposed acquisition, including legal, accounting, and advisory fees.

10. Governing Law
These Heads of Terms and any dispute arising from them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law. The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

Status: These Heads of Terms are not legally binding save for the provisions relating to exclusivity, confidentiality, and costs, which are intended to be legally binding.
