4. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT (Non-Binding)
The Proposed Acquisition is conditional upon:
(a) Satisfactory completion of legal and financial due diligence by the Buyer
(b) Agreement and execution of a formal Sale and Purchase Agreement
(c) Confirmation of funding arrangements satisfactory to the Buyer
(d) No material adverse change in the business, assets, or financial position of the Company prior to Completion

5. DUE DILIGENCE (Non-Binding)
The Buyer will conduct legal, financial, tax, and commercial due diligence on the Company. The Seller agrees to provide all reasonable access to information, personnel, and records as requested. Due diligence will be conducted on a confidential basis and subject to the NDA previously agreed between the parties.

6. TIMETABLE (Non-Binding)
The parties will use reasonable endeavours to achieve completion of the Proposed Acquisition within 12 weeks of the date of this letter, subject to satisfactory completion of due diligence and agreement of the Sale and Purchase Agreement.

7. EXCLUSIVITY (Binding)
In consideration of the Buyer incurring costs in connection with the Proposed Acquisition, the Seller undertakes that for a period of 120 days from the date of this letter the Seller shall not:
(a) solicit, encourage, or entertain any approach from any third party relating to the sale or disposal of the Company or its business or assets
(b) enter into any negotiations or discussions with any third party in relation to such a transaction
(c) provide any information to any third party in connection with any such transaction
This clause 7 is binding on the parties.

8. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION (Non-Binding)
It is intended that the Sale and Purchase Agreement will include undertakings by the Seller that for a period of 2 years following Completion, the Seller will not:
(a) carry on or be engaged or interested in any business which competes with the business of the Company as at Completion
(b) solicit or entice away any employee, customer, or supplier of the Company for a period of 2 years following Completion

9. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNITIES (Non-Binding)
The Sale and Purchase Agreement will include customary warranties from the Seller regarding the Company. It is intended that:
(a) Warranty claims must be notified within 18 months of Completion
(b) The minimum individual claim threshold is £10,000
(c) The aggregate warranty cap will be agreed during the SPA negotiation

10. SALE AND PURCHASE AGREEMENT (Non-Binding)
The Proposed Acquisition will be documented by a formal Sale and Purchase Agreement to be prepared by the Buyer's solicitors.

11. EMPLOYEES (Non-Binding)
The Buyer intends to retain all existing employees of the Company following Completion. The transaction will be subject to applicable employment legislation including TUPE where relevant.

12. COSTS (Binding)
Each party shall bear their own legal and professional costs incurred in connection with the Proposed Acquisition, whether or not the transaction proceeds to Completion. This clause 12 is binding on the parties.

13. ANNOUNCEMENTS (Binding)
Neither party shall make any public announcement or disclosure in connection with the Proposed Acquisition without the prior written consent of the other party. This clause 13 is binding on the parties.

14. GOVERNING LAW (Binding)
This letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. This clause 14 is binding on the parties.
