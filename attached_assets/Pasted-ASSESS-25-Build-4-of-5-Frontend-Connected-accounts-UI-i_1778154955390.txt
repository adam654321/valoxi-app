DECISIONS NEEDED FROM ADAM:

D4.1. Disconnect confirmation:
  (A) Modal AlertDialog with confirm button (current proposal). User
      must click twice to disconnect. Safer.
  (B) One-click disconnect with a toast that has an "Undo" action
      (5-second window before the actual revoke fires).
  Recommended: (A). Disconnect is irreversible (next reconnect
  creates a new grant_id; the old grant data is gone). One-click
  with undo is UX-friendly but adds complexity for a rarely-used
  action.

D4.2. Status pill colours:
  (A) active = green, broken = red. Standard convention.
  (B) active = blue, broken = amber.
  Recommended: (A).

D4.3. Provider naming/icons:
  (A) "Google" + Google G icon, "Microsoft" + Microsoft logo
  (B) "Gmail" / "Outlook" (more user-recognisable than provider
      names)
  Recommended: (B). Users think in terms of where their email lives,
  not the OAuth provider name. "Gmail" / "Outlook" maps cleanly.

D4.4. Should we display the scopes granted to each connected account?
  (A) Yes, expandable details showing "What can Valoxi do with this
      account": Send emails on your behalf, read your name and email
      address. Transparent.
  (B) No. Users don't typically read these. Keep UI clean.
  Recommended: (A) but as a collapsed disclosure ("View permissions"
  link that expands), not always-shown. Builds trust without
  cluttering the main UI.

D4.5. Where in account settings does this live?
  Depends on T1 findings. Three patterns possible:
  (a) New top-level "Connected accounts" or "Integrations" section
      in settings sidebar/tabs.
  (b) New section within existing "Account" or "Profile" page,
      below other account info.
  (c) New sub-route /settings/connected-accounts.
  Recommended: pick the pattern that matches what's already there
  (T1 surfaces). Don't introduce a new convention.
